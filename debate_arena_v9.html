Rules of the Arena

01 · Choose your side

Join Defenders to support the statement or Attackers to challenge it. Your choice is locked for the whole season.

02 · Argue the topic

Stay on the weekly question. Explain your reasoning instead of posting insults.

03 · Support your claims

Use examples and trustworthy sources where possible. Never invent a citation.

04 · Respect other debaters

No harassment, hate, threats, doxxing, or sharing private information.

05 · One fair review

Arguments are assessed using the same rubric. AI suggestions are not automatically the verdict.

06 · Weekly close

The host closes submissions, reviews both sides, and publishes exactly one champion after the seven-day period.
